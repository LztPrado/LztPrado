## Academic Publication

### The Development of Structuring Digital Systems in Brazil

Research article published in *Coletânea de artigos do Colégio Adventista de Bragança Paulista: Volume III*, by Atena Editora (2026).

The research and writing were independently developed by me, with academic guidance from the participating professors.

The article examines the role of structured digital systems in modernizing Brazilian public administration, addressing topics such as efficiency, accessibility, data reliability and digital infrastructure.

- 📄 [Read the published article](https://atenaeditora.com.br/catalogo/post/o-desenvolvimento-de-sistemas-digitais-estruturantes-no-brasil)
- 🔗 [DOI: 10.22533/at.ed.0222619105](https://doi.org/10.22533/at.ed.0222619105)
- 🏛️ Publisher: Atena Editora
